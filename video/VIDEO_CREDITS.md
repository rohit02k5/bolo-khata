## Footage
Every app shot: Playwright screen recordings of the live deployed app.
Hero + ask-back beats re-recorded 2026-11-10 against the production
deployment (bolo-khata.onrender.com) so the on-screen confirm link shows
the real public URL; other beats recorded earlier on the tunnel.
Real browser WebSocket → real Prisma STT + real Timbre TTS.
No mocked UI, no ?say= hook, no fake transcripts. The ask-back beat uses
the app's real editable-transcript feature (labelled on-screen); the
hero take shows the real "naya customer — jodein?" attach flow.
