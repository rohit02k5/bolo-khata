## LinkedIn
```
Subah aath baje. Teen customer ek saath. Aur khata? Wahi phata hua
register — aadha likha, aadha yaad.

Bolo Khata is a voice-first business ledger for India's neighbourhood
shops. The shopkeeper speaks in Hinglish or Telugu (even with the fan
and traffic going), and the bill, stock, khata and reminders update —
with a two-sided confirmation so both sides keep the same record.

What this 90-second brand film shows (all real app footage):
• Voice → bill in one take: "2 kilo cheeni, 1 Maggi, 500 gram dal,
  Ramesh ke naam" → ₹174, via Gnani Prisma speech-to-text
• Customer gets the same bill on their phone and confirms or disputes
• Unknown items ask back instead of guessing
• Cash / UPI QR (self-reported) / Khata, with stock auto-decrement
• Spoken payment reminders, staff roles, weekly spoken insights, and
  business packs for kirana, doodh, akhbaar and more
• 504 noisy-channel benchmark runs — public, failures included

Built at IIT Bhilai. Built with Gnani AI: Prisma + Timbre.

#GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge #GnaniAI
```
